Scope: This notice covers the public website equilens.io. The Equilens FL-BSA appliance runs entirely in customers' environments and is not covered here.

What we collect

  • Emails you send us at hello@equilens.io.
  • Minimal server/CDN logs (IP address, user agent, timestamp, requested URL) required to deliver the site and investigate security issues.
  • Aggregate website analytics from Plausible Analytics: page path, referrer, device type, browser, operating system, and country-level location. Plausible does not use cookies, browser storage, or persistent identifiers; raw IP addresses and full user-agent strings are not stored.

Purposes & legal bases

We process this limited data to respond to inquiries, operate a reliable and secure website, and understand aggregate site usage. The legal basis is our legitimate interests (UK GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)).

Recipients

Personal data may be processed by our service providers strictly for the purposes above, including:

  • Email provider (Google Workspace).
  • Static hosting/CDN provider (GitHub Pages / GitHub, Inc.).
  • Website analytics provider (Plausible Analytics / Plausible Insights OÜ).

International transfers

Where these providers process data outside the UK/EEA (e.g., United States), we rely on appropriate safeguards such as UK adequacy decisions or standard contractual clauses. Details are available on request.

Retention

  • Email correspondence: retained as needed for support and communications, then deleted when no longer required.
  • Server/CDN logs: rotated automatically and retained for approximately 30 days for diagnostics and security.
  • Aggregate Plausible analytics: retained while needed for trend reporting and deleted when no longer required or when the site analytics account is removed.

Your rights

You have UK/EU data protection rights (access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection, portability where applicable). To exercise these rights, email hello@equilens.io.

You may lodge a complaint with the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

Cookies & similar technologies

We use Plausible Analytics for aggregate, cookieless website measurement. We do not use advertising or social media cookies. See our Cookie Policy for details.
