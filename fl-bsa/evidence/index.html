FL-BSA · Customer-hosted pilot

Measure bias amplification in a credit‑decision simulation

One synthetic branch trains with historical decisions, one without. FL-BSA compares their group approval‑rate gaps inside your environment and keeps a traceable evidence trail.

Get the sample evidence report

PDF, 16 pages, 246 KB · synthetic demo scenario

What the evidence shows

  • Amplification, set against a baseline

    The Δ gap separates the approval-rate gap learned from historical decisions from the gap produced by your configured decision rules alone.

  • Your data stays with you

    In the self-hosted deployment, borrower datasets, model parameters and protected-attribute data stay within your VPC or VM.

  • Evidence your team can verify

    Each run delivers a report, metrics and a SHA‑256 hash-linked manifest recording the dataset hash, random seed and software version. Verification works offline.

What an evaluation involves

One regulated-credit workflow over 6 to 10 weeks, hosted in your environment: an agreed question and data boundary, one supported simulation, and a governance readout for your responsible team to review.

Discuss an evaluation

FL-BSA simulates decision patterns over synthetic borrower cohorts and configured rules, so it works without access to your production models. The sample report uses the balanced public demo scenario. Read the full product overview.
