FL-BSA · Customer-hosted pilot

Do your past credit decisions amplify bias?

FL-BSA compares the approval‑rate gap learned from your recorded decisions with the gap from a baseline built without them, over synthetic borrower cohorts in your own environment, so borrower and protected‑attribute data stay with you.

What the evidence shows

  • Amplified by history, or already in the rules

    One branch learns from your recorded decisions; the other trains without them and applies configured fair‑decision rules to the same synthetic cohort. The difference between their group approval‑rate gaps is the amplification your history adds.

  • Your data stays with you

    In the self-hosted deployment, borrower datasets, model parameters and protected-attribute data stay within your VPC or VM.

  • Evidence your team can verify

    Each run delivers a report, metrics and a hash‑linked (SHA‑256) manifest recording the dataset hash, random seed and software version. Your team can verify it offline.

EU AI Act Article 4a includes whether bias detection and correction can be achieved effectively with other data, including synthetic or anonymised data, among the conditions for its exceptional special‑category‑data route. An evaluation records what the simulation establishes and what it does not.

What an evaluation involves

One regulated-credit workflow over 6 to 10 weeks, hosted in your environment: an agreed question and data boundary, one simulation run with Equilens support, and a governance readout for your responsible team to review.

Discuss an evaluation

FL-BSA simulates decision patterns over synthetic borrower cohorts and configured rules, so it works without access to your production models.
