A bounded, reviewable process

How it works

FL-BSA (Fair-Lending Bias-Simulation Appliance) connects a simulation question to the assumptions and evidence needed to review its result.

  1. 1

    Agree the scenario

    Define the credit workflow, population, evidence question and approved synthetic input boundary before running a test.

  2. 2

    Run the simulation

    Compare decision patterns across synthetic borrower groups inside your environment. Record the configuration and assumptions with the outputs.

  3. 3

    Review the evidence

    Inspect the report, metrics and manifest. Verify bundle consistency, record limitations and agree the next action with the responsible team.

EU AI Act timing and the synthetic-data question

The high-risk rules for relevant creditworthiness and credit-score systems are due to apply from 2 December 2027.

EU AI Act Article 4a includes whether bias detection and correction can be achieved effectively with other data, including synthetic or anonymised data, among the conditions for its exceptional special-category-data route. An evaluation records what the simulation establishes and what it does not.

Common questions

Does FL-BSA test our actual production model?

No. The current product simulates decision patterns over synthetic borrower cohorts. It does not load or execute your models or scorecards. Measurement over customer-authored decision or score columns is a separate development track and is not part of the current release.

What is included in the evidence bundle?

A PDF report, metrics, an evidence manifest recording the dataset hash, random seed and software version, and certificate appendices. These are technical evidence artifacts, not regulatory certifications.

Does any customer data leave our environment?

In the configured self-hosted deployment, compute stays in your VPC or VM. Borrower datasets, model parameters and protected-attribute data are not sent to Equilens-controlled services. Any optional usage metering is configured separately.

How can FL-BSA be deployed?

The intended AWS path is AMI-first. Supported container or VM paths may be agreed separately. Public AWS Marketplace access is not yet available. The selected native v5 runtime is CPU-based; GPU acceleration is not part of that runtime.

How long does a typical run take?

There is no published validated exact-version native sizing baseline or customer SLA. Measure the delivered artifact on the target hardware. Runtime depends on input shape, hardware, configuration and enabled validation work.

Can we verify the evidence ourselves?

Yes. Bundles are hash-linked using SHA-256 and include a hashing specification. Where signatures are enabled, vendor-authored attestations can be verified against the published vendor trust root. Customer-local bundle consistency does not by itself prove vendor authorship. Verification can be performed offline.
