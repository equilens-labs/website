Use case: illustration-story.
Asset: one finished homepage hero illustration for Equilens FL-BSA, an enterprise tool that compares synthetic lending-decision patterns and produces evidence for human review.
Reference image: use ONLY its light indigo/white palette, meticulous editorial 3D craft, paper textures, restrained transparency and soft shadows. Replace its entire subject and composition. The reference's abstract glass panels and cables were rejected as meaningless. A subsequent numerical table was also rejected. We need an actual illustration whose visual story explains the product.
Story to tell: "Make synthetic borrowers, reveal a difference between groups, retain the evidence." Show this in ONE coherent sculptural scene, not three boxed panels.
Scene and composition: near-square 5:4 landscape canvas, pure white seamless background with generous breathing room. Elevated three-quarter view. On the left is a small elegant fan of three paper borrower-profile cards, recognisable by simple head-and-shoulders silhouettes and just a few embossed form lines. A delicate indigo trail leads from these cards into TWO adjacent shallow white paper trays in the centre. Each tray holds a small cluster of five freestanding paper-cut borrower-profile figures, arranged naturally with depth rather than a spreadsheet grid. The figures have identical neutral human silhouettes, no gender stereotypes. In one tray several figures have prominent indigo approval-check tabs at their bases; in the other only a couple do, with the rest having pale grey neutral dash tabs. This visibly unequal pattern is the central content.
A refined, slender dark-indigo-rimmed magnifying glass is suspended diagonally above and across the boundary between the two trays, its clear lens enlarging the outcome tabs beneath it. It must visibly examine the two groups, not float pointlessly in space. No hand holding it. The lens and the human groups together are the focal point. The two trays remain clearly distinct and legible.
On the right, connected to the examined area by one fine indigo trace, a pair of beautiful slightly offset report sheets retain a tiny pictorial echo of those two groups and a highlighted difference. Just a few short embossed rules and small linked marks suggest the evidence trail. The paper report is secondary and clearly a document, never a dashboard or table. NO bars, axes, percentages or statistical display. Keep the complete story compact and visually connected; no long cable spaghetti.
Style: bespoke high-end editorial illustration, precise dimensional paper engineering and restrained optical glass, soft matte indigo details, clean silhouettes, subtle tactile materials. Sophisticated and inviting, not childish clay toys, not stock business clip-art. The image should be understandable at 500px and remain visually strong at 320px.
Palette: white and pale cool grey, deep navy #111827, indigo #4F46E5 and soft lavender. White background all the way to the image edges, soft realistic contact shadows. No dark enclosing panel, neon glow or purple fog.
Text: NO text, letters, numbers, logo or watermark. We will add three short readable labels as real webpage text. All human figures and outcome markers are schematic synthetic examples, not actual results or a claim about a customer. Approval ticks belong ONLY to individual simulated borrower outcomes; no global certification badge, regulatory seal or compliance checkmark.
Avoid: abstract processing blocks, glass servers, software screens, rounded UI cards, data tables, charts, financial currency symbols, scales of justice, robots, machines making live credit decisions. Do not create a website screenshot. Make a distinctive narrative illustration.
