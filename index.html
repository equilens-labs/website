Fair-Outcomes Evidence

FL-BSA is a self-hosted evidence appliance for regulated credit decisions: simulate decision patterns, package tamper-evident evidence packs, and keep borrower data in your environment.

Explore FL-BSA Review Trust Center

Key FL-BSA capabilities

Outcome Simulation

Generate synthetic borrower scenarios and stress-test lending decision patterns for disparity before concerns become live remediation work.

Evidence Packs

Turn each run into reports, manifests, certificates, and documentation that risk, compliance, and audit teams can review together.

Customer-Hosted

Deploy FL-BSA inside your own environment so sensitive data, model artifacts, and review workflows stay within your perimeter.
