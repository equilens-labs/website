Presenting FL-BSA

FL-BSA (Fair-Lending Bias-Simulation Appliance) is our flagship product: simulate decision patterns, package tamper-evident evidence packs, and keep borrower data in your environment.

Outcome simulation

Generate synthetic borrower scenarios and stress-test lending decision patterns for disparity before concerns become live remediation work.

Evidence packs

Turn each run into reports, manifests, certificates, and documentation that risk, compliance, and audit teams review together.

Customer-hosted

Deploy FL-BSA inside your own environment so sensitive data, model artifacts, and review workflows stay within your perimeter.

See how FL-BSA works

Why self-hosted?

Your data stays yours

Most fairness analytics ask you to send decision data to a vendor's cloud. FL-BSA deploys into your own environment, with no-egress enforced in our release pipeline.

No live decisions, ever

FL-BSA never makes or influences a live lending decision. It simulates and measures, without touching production.

Verifiable evidence

Every evidence pack is hash-chained, so your auditors can verify it independently against our published trust root.

The EU AI Act applies to high-risk credit scoring from 2 August 2026 and asks for documented data governance and traceability. FL-BSA is built to help you prepare that evidence. This is not legal advice, and FL-BSA does not certify compliance.

Verify it yourself

Everything we publish ships with SHA-256 manifests and a published trust root. Download the demo artifacts and recompute the hashes yourself.
