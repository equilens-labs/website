Why teams choose a self-hosted appliance

Most fairness analytics ask you to send decision data to a vendor's cloud. FL-BSA works the other way round: it deploys into your own environment, with no-egress enforced in our release pipeline, and it never makes or influences a live lending decision. Every evidence pack is hash-chained, so your auditors can verify it independently — without trusting us.

The EU AI Act applies to high-risk credit scoring from 2 August 2026 and asks for documented data governance and traceability. FL-BSA is built to help you prepare that evidence. This is not legal advice, and FL-BSA does not certify compliance.
